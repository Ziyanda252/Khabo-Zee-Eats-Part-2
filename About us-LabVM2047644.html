Khabo Zee's Eats was established on October 2020 with a desire to bring traditional flavours to our customers. What started as a love for hearty dishes grew into a business dedicated to serving meals that remind our customers of home.

Our mission

Our mission is not only to make a profit, but to ensure that our customers receive high-quality food at reasonable prices. We aim to provide a diverse menu that caters to different tastes and preferences while delivering food that is prepared with care and warmth.

Our vision

We wish to expand out business over the years, making our traditional food accessible to customers in other cities while continuing to provide the same quality, warmth, and taste that our customers know and love.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Khabo Zee's Eats special?

We celebrate traditional South African food and the flavours that bring people together.

What food do you offer?

Our menu includes inyama yenhloko, pork trotters, pap and mogodu, and stuffed fatcakes.

Where can we find you?

You can find us at Vilakazi Street, Soweto, South Africa.
